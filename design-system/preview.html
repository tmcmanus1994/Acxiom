Core

Color

Four core colors carry the brand. Text is black or white only — the accents are for data, diagrams, and highlights, never for type. Bone is not white: do not substitute #FFFFFF for #EDECE8 as a background.

The glow

Typography

Tw Cen MT carries headlines and body. Consolas is the kicker voice — always uppercase, always widely tracked, never body copy. Sizes are points on the 960 × 540pt canvas.
